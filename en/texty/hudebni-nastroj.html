MUSICAL INSTRUMENT

essay 2026 written: in my head
Entertain US
Let’s not shout! Let’s not shout! Let’s not shout!
You wouldn’t like it either!

I used to play the guitar, but if I had to choose an instrument I’d want to play perfectly, it would be singing – the voice. Actually it doesn’t even have to be perfect, or even good, to be beautiful. You can vote with a horrible voice too, right (as long as you’re 18…). In my head it sounds more like irony than a metaphor.

Maybe it’s the voice of a schizophrenic / a madman / an angel

I don’t know if the “perfect voice” is the one thinking and talking in my head right now, or some deep singer’s voice. Maybe it’s the voice of the people I love, calling me somewhere – or maybe the voice of that angel.

angel? ← Nonsense

But does it matter, when “democratically” we can all vote? Cast our votes with different voices? Different tones, colours and volumes. I weigh the same as that angel.
