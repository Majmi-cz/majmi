WHO WILL HOLD PÉŤA?

dialogue 2026
It’s not about kindergarten! ⚠ ⚠ ⚠

characters

Tonda – a typical kindergarten bully.

Péťa – the victim, a year younger.

Věra – also a kindergartner.


setting

A sandpit by the kindergarten, with no teachers or parents watching.

(Around noon the teachers take the preschoolers out to the playground. Usually nobody plays in the far corner of it, because the teachers can’t see that far. Today, though, Péťa had the bad luck that his ball rolled over there just when Tonda and his gang were there.)

PéťaH-h-hi, guys…

TondaWhat do you want, shrimp?

(Tonda immediately takes up a position to hit little Péťa just for the sake of it. Then he notices the slowly rolling ball.)

[5] = Pět (five) = Péťa

TondaThat yours?

PéťaYes… that’s my… ball.

TondaHere, you little shit.

(Tonda picks up the ball and, without a shred of guilt, throws it at Péťa as hard as he can, to make him cry and leave a bruise.)

PéťaOw… you didn’t… have t–

TondaBut I did, and I want to. Besides, who’s gonna stop me? The teacher? She can’t see this far, the stupid cow.

PéťaDo you enjoy hurting me?

TondaYeah, a lot. Thanks for asking, clown.

(Věra catches sight of what’s happening and, being curious, comes over to ask what’s going on.)

VěraHi, guys, what happened–

TondaGive me that, dumbass.

(Tonda snatches the ball from Péťa and throws it at Věra too, who is “snooping” around.)

TondaThere you go, cow!

VěraWhy… would you–

(Věra gasps for breath and is close to tears.)

JARmila?
NOT ME

TondaGet out of my sight, lovebirds!

(Péťa, although let down and sad, takes Věra, who is already in tears, and leads her away, off the playground, without crying himself.)

VěraBut we’re not allowed here–

PéťaLook, I don’t care…

VěraWell… thanks, then.

(Věra lets Péťa hold her and hug her. Tears run down her cheeks and leave a wet patch on Péťa’s shoulder.)

PéťaDon’t worry, it’ll stop hurting… soon.

VěraBut why would he do that? Just spread hate for no reason? Why? Why?

PéťaShh… shh… calm down. It’s obvious and unavoidable that in a role like that he’ll do things like that… Don’t sob.

VěraYou’re defending him?! Why would you say that? It’s foolish to say he’s allowed.

PéťaHe’s a person. And of course he’ll use his role and his chances.

VěraYou can’t mean that! You don’t even believe it yourself!

PéťaVěra! Stay here!

(Věra, convinced that Péťa is on the side of Tonda’s gang, cries even harder and runs back across the playground to the teachers.)

PéťaWho was the last one to hold me like that? Doesn’t matter. She’s right, I don’t believe it myself. But I have to. Otherwise I’d start crying too. It’s easier to hold someone else than to hold yourself.

(Although Věra reached the teachers sobbing and in tears that the boys were bullying each other and being mean, she didn’t mention Péťa’s name. She doesn’t want to hurt him by telling on them. But the teachers might not care, now that they suddenly see a child off the playground.)
