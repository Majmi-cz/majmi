WHEN THE PAPER YELLOWS

poem 2026

When the paper yellows, the head won’t remember and reason disappears, the heart will stay pure. The oldest symbol of love, the body’s dearest organ. When yours stops beating, there’ll be no help for me. I’d have to go too, without you I can’t dream. I’ll be like Juliet sipping the poison.
