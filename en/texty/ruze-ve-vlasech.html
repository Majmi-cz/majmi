ROSES IN HER HAIR

poem 2026 written: in my head

In their hair there lies a rose, no one can take it off. It lies there in the hair, I would be afraid to take it off. Her curls are tangled with thorns and how to change that, I don’t know. I don’t know how to solve the rose, I’ll ask a wiser man. Foolishly I touch the flower and it didn’t destroy my world. I thought it would hurt, but she chooses her own mistakes. So I asked the man whether it’s a bad rose. He told me to look at my mother, she wears a lovely flower too. Women wear “roses” and no one will help you with that. They will stay there forever, they are beautiful truths.
