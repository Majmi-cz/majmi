WHY

Yesterday I was born, today I write, tomorrow I am loved and the day after I wallow. In money, I’d prefer; in tears.

I don’t think it’s a tragedy – it’s beautiful. The pages of my plain poetry will yellow and be forgotten too, or someone will burn them.

That is exactly what this is about. The suffering you fall in love with while growing up – Love.

This isn’t a magazine. It’s a notebook you can read from the outside.

Everything is written by hand first, crossed-out words and all, and only then copied out here. That’s why every text comes with its manuscript.

Some things are finished, some aren’t, and with some of it, a year from now, I probably won’t agree. That’s part of it.
