POEMS

Six-Petal Daisy

She loves me, she loves me not, I ask her come the morning. She asks what will become of us, the young lady asks. So she loves me, since she has such a good friend. Why else would she bring handkerchiefs down to the river. So she loves me not, she’s grown fond of the word flaw. She tells me, repeats it, reminds me how miserable my next line is. The daisy has no more petals. I should have just let it lie. Not picked it, not read it, let it bloom, or else it could lead me astray. Now I refuse to set foot in the garden again, I would hear the same voice.

Сожаление (Regret)

No red is crimson enough to describe her lips. No sculptor can choose a marble fine enough or a granite rough enough. No carpenter will find a willow dry enough for the finest Easter switch, nor an oak that keeps its leaves in winter, nor a birch that won’t burst into passion. The highest mountain and the longest river don’t know what such beauty means. The lights of night cities can’t reach as far as the stars, and even those don’t come up to her ankles. No government can promise you enough, even though you’re modest. Though they dig and dig into the till, her worth is beyond counting. No author will ever save Werther and no colour will make Lucifer stop weeping. The Lord God sent His Son Jesus years ago, but today He sent me an angel. Just for me, so that I could look and not catch it in words.

Roses in Her Hair

In their hair there lies a rose, no one can take it off. It lies there in the hair, I would be afraid to take it off. Her curls are tangled with thorns and how to change that, I don’t know. I don’t know how to solve the rose, I’ll ask a wiser man. Foolishly I touch the flower and it didn’t destroy my world. I thought it would hurt, but she chooses her own mistakes. So I asked the man whether it’s a bad rose. He told me to look at my mother, she wears a lovely flower too. Women wear “roses” and no one will help you with that. They will stay there forever, they are beautiful truths.

The Letter

Once again I write a long letter and can’t manage to describe you. You’re beautiful, I suppose, and I’m at rock bottom. Walk past so gracefully one more time and I’ll end myself. You can’t torment me this long, I want to collect memories with you. You never gave me a single chance, can’t we just sit down? Talk it out between us, I want us to be alone. I feel like sending it unsigned, maybe that will make the letter matter more. You don’t even read the text, let alone the words. A lost cause, I’m done. I don’t feel like anything, I’m drinking the mercury from a thermometer. You are a dictator and a tyrant, held tight in your arms. Stop it, I’ve been crying in vain too long. It could never have turned out well. A lost cause from the very start, thinking I’d call you darling. Execute me in public, go ahead, I don’t care, apparently. The heart burns, the heart squeezes and my hope is dying. So fast, so young, for people like me there is no advice. I am one of the cursed poets, born in the wrong year. But you don’t even know how it writes itself and still my children don’t call you mum. With that I’ll end the thought of this letter, I won’t write anything more. No appetite, no strength, no will, screw it, man.

When the Paper Yellows

When the paper yellows, the head won’t remember and reason disappears, the heart will stay pure. The oldest symbol of love, the body’s dearest organ. When yours stops beating, there’ll be no help for me. I’d have to go too, without you I can’t dream. I’ll be like Juliet sipping the poison.

He Shot Himself on the First of May

He shot himself on the first of May, a young man going to paradise. The First of May, the time of love, one voice was lost. On the first day nobody knew where the young man had gone. On the first day nobody knows where the young man sleeps. On the third day a letter was found and on it the young man’s signature. It said that he felt miserable, things didn’t look hopeful for him. Loss is a race over a terribly long track, that’s why his mother wailed “come back now”. But even she stopped sobbing, stopped visiting the grave. Now nobody knows anything, the young man is gone. A year went by – everyone forgot, but they celebrated the First of May properly. The First of May, the time of love. Suddenly one voice was missing…

One of the Cursed Poets

One of the cursed poets devotes himself to past years. Once it was a delight, a plaster on the heart, to receive someone else’s poem. But today that is fading. Today I write, today you read and still you scream at it. I wrote too late, right away – is too late. I wish it were like it used to be, to write you letters, poems like Wolker, and I wouldn’t be a stalker. Today they say creepy, they used to say “sweet”. It would be easy to love back like in 1925. Say that you want to be loved and you will be dishonoured.
