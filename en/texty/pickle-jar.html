PICKLE JAR

dialogue 2026

characters

Jan Novák – the man of the house, though tall ⇒ thin ⇒ weak. Young, a nerd, not enough.

Jarmila Nováková – a considerably stronger, sturdier, if shorter young wife. She’d rather give orders than do it herself.


setting

Kitchen / dining room. It’s just before dinner, both partners are tired from work, and although the omelette is ready on the table, they both want pickles with it.

(The smell of a fresh dinner drifts through the dining room. An omelette, the simplest way to cook eggs. The newlyweds meet at the table.)

JanI’m unbelievably hungry. I’m looking forward to this more than to you, maybe.

(An ironic smile follows.)

JarmilaYou’re as funny as breakfast. But did you notice anything?

JanMe? You don’t have new hair.

JarmilaNot everything is about me, either.

JanThen what do you mean? I’m completely lost…

JarmilaI do admire that you can cook and I don’t have to, like always, but darling, come on – where’s the side? Bread, rolls, a loaf, or vegetables – peppers and pickles?

JanWell, I’m convinced an omelette is enough on its own.

JarmilaIt’s always enough for you…

(Jarmila gets up from the dining table and goes to the kitchen to find something to liven up her dinner.)

JanDarling, don’t be cross, that’s just how I am.

JarmilaI am well aware that that’s how you are.

(Jan gets up and in the kitchen immediately hands the jar to Jarmila.)

JarmilaThank you.

JanYou’re welcome.

JarmilaOF COURSE I’m welcome. Whatever happened to being a gentleman?

JanAre you talking about some standard again that I don’t get or that doesn’t seem adequate to me?

JarmilaAdequate? All you have to do is open a jar of pickles for a lady. Just get the lid off, gentleman.

JanYeah? Well, sorry for thinking logically and not considering who goes to the gym three times a week and who doesn’t? You’re the stronger one.

JarmilaBut you’re the man.

JanYou’re the woman. What’s that supposed to mean.

(Jarmila hands Jan the jar.)

JarmilaConvince me.

JanOf what?

JarmilaThat you’re not the “wonderful wife” you so love pampering yourself as.

JanDon’t you think you’re being sexist?

JarmilaI think I’m being the husband.

(Jan can’t believe what he’s hearing. Yes, he loves Jarmila, but arguing any further would feel embarrassing, so he starts opening the jar of pickles.)

JarmilaIt won’t open.

Jan…It… won’t…

JarmilaDoes that seem right to you?

JanIt’s ironic, but yes.

(Jarmila opens the jar of pickles without any trouble.)

JarmilaI know you can argue, but explain this: am I more of a man, or are you more of a woman?

JanNeither.

JarmilaSo what’s the answer to my question, smart guy?

JanYou have oestrogen, I have testosterone. Neither hormone changes whether I should or shouldn’t – whether I actually CAN open a jar, or a door, for that matter.

(Jarmila thinks for a moment. Then the couple returns to the table and carries on with dinner. They never come back to the conversation, though. It’s an unimportant argument at the start of a marriage.)
