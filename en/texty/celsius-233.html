CELSIUS 233

essay 2026 written: in my head
233°C → round it!!

Do I even enjoy reading? Do I read because I like it, or because I want to be “the one who knows”?

The last thing I finished was Goethe’s Werther and I can’t get over it. Not over the idea that angels walk among people, as R. Williams said playing Sean. And that people like me are willing to kill themselves over that selfishness. What gets me is that I knew how it ends before I even bought the book.

Spoiler alert! ⚠

So the real question isn’t whether or not I’m a performative reader. The question is: do I want to do something without knowing how it ends? In the world of literature, at least, I’ve probably never read a book without knowing how it ends. Do I need that kind of certainty in my personal life too?

New complain
What are the lines past the margin for?

There’s a psychological phenomenon where people prefer to receive love randomly rather than consistently. I don’t know where I read or heard it, but I think I agree. Does that mean I’m not human, then? That’s nonsense. If I put it into this equation, it rather means that I don’t really enjoy reading. Do I still want to call myself a reader? Probably yes. Being performative, putting on a show, wearing a plastic mask, playing the puppeteer and being the puppet – that defines humanity, or at least society.

Free space.
Poor tree.
Seashells are amazing

I wonder whether I actually enjoy anything, whether I only have hobbies so I can answer “What did you do at the weekend?” without an awkward silence.

All Apologies );(

I can’t put on a film without googling halfway through how it ends or what the point actually is. Comfort is a world. I probably couldn’t start a new hobby. It would fundamentally change “who I am”.

* Or Wilde?
Who cares

Whitman* was right when he said we need to call ourselves something, to fit in somewhere, to be something. He was right that a person is a verb and not a noun. But today? One whiff of comfort and I have to know what I’m getting into. Without that nobody, me least of all, has the courage – never mind courage, not even a reason.

Maybe watching my parents spoiled it for me, and that’s why I’m here – a stable job, a family at 30, the corporate ladder… We all know it, I hope.
