FAME X STRAW

essay 2026 written: in my head
is not the only one
Something in the way

Who’s better off? Me, who sits at home, turns on LoL and plays like a peasant, or being world champion and doing it as a job? See bilibili. You have to enjoy it all the time, but this isn’t about LoL. When a farmer finishes his graft and lies down to sleep on a pile of hay or straw, he gets dopamine from the work, and besides that very dopamine warming his heart, outside the pile warms him. Can a celebrity say that? They have to be scared of saying anything, so they don’t leave someone out, forget someone or, worst of all, offend someone. How can that be rewarding for anyone? No amount of money will save that fame and influence. And work is a fortress. A cold one, and above all, those who are outside want out, and vice versa. It’s like the one-sided relationship “we young people” have every day. – and okay, in my case, but whatever. The point stands – Even though the gold a celebrity gets paid is as cold as a stone on the road you walk.
